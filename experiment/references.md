### Textbooks

1. "Principles of CMOS VLSI Design" by Weste & Eshraghian
   - Comprehensive coverage of CMOS logic, including pass transistor logic and transmission gates.
2. "System Integration: From Transistor Design to Large Scale Integration" by Kurt Hoffman
   - Explores integration techniques and transistor-level design, relevant for understanding pass transistor logic.
3. "Electronics (Fundamentals and Applications)" by D. Chattopadhyay
   - Foundational electronics concepts, MOSFET operation, and logic gate design.
4. "The SPICE Book" by Andrei Vladimirescu, John Wiley & Sons, Inc.
   - Authoritative guide to SPICE simulation, device modeling, and practical circuit analysis.
5. "A Guide to Circuit Simulation and Analysis Using PSpice" by Paul Tuinenga, 3rd Edition, Prentice-Hall.
   - Step-by-step instructions for SPICE-based simulation, including MOSFET and logic circuits.

### SPICE User Manuals & Tutorials

6. [SPICE Tutorial: University of Texas](http://users.ece.utexas.edu/~adnan/vlsi-05-backup/lec7Spice.ppt)
   - Slide deck introducing SPICE syntax, device models, and simulation examples for digital logic.
7. [ngspice User Manual](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/docs/ngspice-manual.pdf)
   - Official documentation for ngspice, including MOSFET modeling and simulation commands.

### Applications & Advanced Topics

8. "Low-Power CMOS Digital Design" by Anantha Chandrakasan, IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits, 1995.
   - Discusses low-power design techniques, including pass transistor logic and threshold drop mitigation.
9. "Pass-Transistor Logic: Design and Applications" by J. Yuan and C. Svensson, IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits, 1989.
   - Seminal paper on pass transistor logic, design challenges, and practical applications.

### Open-Source Tools

10. [ngspice](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/)
    - Widely used open-source SPICE simulator for analog and digital circuit analysis.
11. [LTspice](https://www.analog.com/en/design-center/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html)
    - Free SPICE simulator with advanced features for MOSFET and logic gate simulation.

### Web Resources

12. [All About Circuits: Pass Transistor Logic](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/technical-articles/pass-transistor-logic/)
    - Overview of pass transistor logic, threshold voltage issues, and design strategies.
13. [CMOS Transmission Gates and Pass Transistor Logic](https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/logic/logic_7.html)
    - Tutorial on transmission gates, pass transistor logic, and their use in digital circuits.
